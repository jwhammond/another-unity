The transporter room now has every painted button as a control: the lists and map buttons, the scroll arrows for the lists and for the kit, the preview box, the sliders (Energize), and the exit door. The kit is a tray of four slots with the round arrow, as in the original. The map is built from the game's own pictures, with its scan lines and the lock-on, and the scan sound plays.

Still different: Recommended, which suggests a landing party, has no button in the original and sits under the exits in ours.
