The zoo is a wildlife preserve with eleven rooms with art, and the world with the most small animated props: creatures, lights, maps and sensors.

## The look

Habitats, tanks and field equipment. Each plate is de-dithered, enlarged four times, repainted and checked against the original within 2 px. All eleven passed as repainted plates, and one of them needed the near-lossless encoding.

## Not a room

The original also lists an object-only entry for this world, a bundle of advice triggers. It has no background, so it is not shown.

## What animates

About thirty props, more than any other world. Among them a jellybird, a monkey, worms, coral, the habitat lights, scanners for the animals, a shuttle, a door and several maps and screens.

Most of these were handled the same way: where the repaint added speckle that made the frames flicker, the prop keeps a plain upscale instead of a repaint. That is why the animated layers look slightly crisper and less painted than the plates around them. Three layers, a flock and two terrain maps, failed the alignment check twice and have no animation yet.

## Characters

The creatures and the staff have stills and portraits rather than moving figures, drawn from the same pipeline as the others. The sheet here collects a dozen of the world's own cards: animals, scanners and the people who work there.
