Allanor is a drone-run ship with nineteen rooms with art: long corridors, doors and a lot of coloured buttons.

## The look

Machine interiors, heavy metal and warning colours. Plates are de-dithered, enlarged four times and repainted, then checked against the original within 2 px. Most passed as they were.

## Rooms that took a different route

- One room needed a small local warp, a few points moved back into line, and then passed.
- One room, the seventh, is upscale-only. The repaint drifted a point by 6 to 7.5 px, beyond what the warp is allowed to correct, so by ruling it keeps a plate that is only enlarged. It still passed the check.
- Room S14 is upscale-only too, but for a different reason: its original is a flat grey with a single horizontal black band. There is no edge that can measure the horizontal axis, so no plate could pass the check at all. The recipe records this as a skipped check with the reason, rather than as a failure.
- Two rooms in the original's list, the second and the last, are cut: the files holding their backgrounds are in neither source, so there is nothing to show.

Two of the plates needed the near-lossless encoding because the usual one drifted from its source.

## The cards

The first sheet gathers the world's own cards: the drones, the optimators, the Chodak master, the admiral and the crawler, then a few props.

## What animates

Doors, the numbered buttons that light and unlight, a ventilation duct and a security rod whose two animations the script swaps. The second sheet is the Chodak's own beam effect, upscaled only. Several characters, the admiral, the Chodak master, the drones and the optimators, have still cards or portraits rather than animations.

One quirk: the Chodak master and the optimators in room S0D are drawn by the original at about two 256ths of their size, because that is the floor's scale at the horizon. That is under a pixel, so nothing is shown for them in either version.
