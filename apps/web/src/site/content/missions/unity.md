Unity is the last mission: sixteen rooms with art, all repainted, and the largest animated layers in the game.

## The look

An ancient alien complex, lit by energy and glow. Every plate is de-dithered, enlarged four times, repainted and checked within 2 px, and every one passed with no warp and no upscale-only fallback.

## What animates

Around thirty layers: force fields, doors, panel buttons, a big sun, smoke, a zap, a key symbol, a glow in the third room and, in the last room, the sun.

The biggest of any room are the two halves of the vortex in the tenth room: together they take about 34 MiB once decoded, well under the budget of 64 MiB per room. The stasis chambers show their first frame as the original's own picture, since a repaint speckled.

## What is missing

Four props failed the alignment check twice (the lights of the second room, two shuttle animations, and a side light) and eight were refused: the shuttlecraft, six balls and the admiral's body. They still draw as the plate has them.

## Characters

The Guardian, the creature, the admiral and the captain have portraits and cards. The sheet here collects a dozen of the world's own cards.
