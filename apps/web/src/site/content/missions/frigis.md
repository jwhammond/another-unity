Frigis is a cold, ruled world with twelve rooms with art, and one room that is a puzzle laid across the floor.

## The look

Volcanic haze, stone and pale metal. Plates are de-dithered, enlarged four times, repainted and checked within 2 px. One room needed a small local warp; one is upscale-only, because its repaint drifted past the warp's limit and by ruling it keeps the plain enlargement. A thirteenth room in the original's list is cut: its background file exists in neither source.

## The floor tiles

The fourth room's puzzle floor is made of twenty-two separate tiles. They are not one repeated sprite: each has its own picture and size, and a script switches them on and off, with eight of them dark when the room starts. Each tile is a still, repainted once, so the floor reads as one surface while every tile still answers to the puzzle.

## What animates

The two doors of that room, one with a 65-frame opening onto a tunnel, four lights, a red light, a pipe's gas, two heat hazes, the volcanoes' haze, the force field, and the two transporter doorways. Several of these are plain upscales, because a repaint made them speckle.

The burning manuscript in the fifth room failed its check twice, so it has no animation published.
