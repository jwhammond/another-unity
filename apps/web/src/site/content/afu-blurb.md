Realized in the new Another Unity engine, this aims to be a faithful recreation of the original 1995 MS-DOS game, meaning some mechanics certainly feel like they are still in 1995 and don't reflect the full capabilities of the engine.

To play, you need your own copy of the original game: the CD, or an image of it. The first time you play, the game asks for it, checks it in your browser and remembers it. Nothing from the disc is uploaded.

It is currently in beta playtests. Any feedback or details on bugs encountered would be appreciated.

Space combat is in, faithful to the original, with assists in the settings: an easier combat level, pause and fast-forward, and auto-resolve.

Features not implemented:

- Upscaled cutscenes. Due to the low quality and early 3D graphics, these will likely remain as the original videos.
