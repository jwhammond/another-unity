## Portraits

Everyone you talk to in the original game has a small head picture beside the dialog box: 48 by 50 pixels, in the game's own limited palette. The remake keeps the idea and replaces the picture. Each new portrait is 64 by 64 pixels. An image model paints a face using the original head as its layout, the result is fitted into a frame, and then it is pixelated again so it sits with the rest of the art. The person stays recognisably the same one.

The crew and the guests are made the same way, and each portrait has a short recipe file that records the prompt and the settings. Drag the slider on a picture to move from the original head, enlarged four times with no smoothing, to the remake.

A few rules came out of looking at the first results.

- Only Starfleet people wear a rank pip, so the recipes for everyone else ask for none and list pips as something to avoid. That does not always work: one of the two published Avakar portraits has a small collar stud, and T'Bak's picture is an earlier one that still has the grey collar and its studs.
- The Garidians are meant to look like Romulans: pointed ears, a heavy brow, upswept eyebrows and a straight fringe. T'Bak's two Romulan attempts came out too much like a Klingon, so he keeps his earlier portrait.
- The non-human and heavily painted heads are repainted without the face-finding tools, because those tools find no face in them. Where a face box was needed it was measured by hand.
